Momo

Welcome to my recipe show. Today we are learning recipe for buff momo.

Ingredients

  • Plain Flour
  • Minced meat of your choice
  • Spices
  • Tomato

Instructions

  1. Knead the dough of flour with pinch of salt and a table spoon of oil.
  2. Let it rest for at lease 30 minutes
  3. For the stuffing, we will take minced buff, finely chopped onions, corriender, and veggies of your choice
  4. Add the required spices of your taste and put it aside.
  5. For achar, we will toss few tomatoes along with few cubes of onion, garlic, ginger and green chilles.
  6. When the tomatoes are soft let it be cooled in room temperature.
  7. Once it is cold then we will grind it into fine paste and add water to get desired consistency.
